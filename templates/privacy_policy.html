Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Sportfolio is a social platform for sports fans to log, rate, review, and remember the games they watch.

Information We Collect

When you create a Sportfolio account, we may collect information such as your username, account information, and profile information that you choose to provide.

Sportfolio also stores content and activity that you choose to add to the platform, including game logs, ratings, reviews, comments, follows, favorite teams, and other profile information.

If you upload a profile picture, that image is stored as part of your Sportfolio profile.

How We Use Information

Information you provide is used to operate and improve Sportfolio, including displaying your profile, game history, social activity, comments, ratings, and other features you choose to use.

Information You Share

Information you choose to make part of your Sportfolio profile or social activity may be visible to other Sportfolio users according to the features and settings of the platform.

Account Information

Your Sportfolio account is associated with the information required to create and maintain your account. We use this information to authenticate you and provide access to your Sportfolio account.

Data Storage

Sportfolio uses third-party hosting and infrastructure services to operate the website and store application data.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as Sportfolio changes or new features are introduced. The most recent version will be posted on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or Sportfolio's handling of information, please contact Sportfolio through the support options provided on the platform.
